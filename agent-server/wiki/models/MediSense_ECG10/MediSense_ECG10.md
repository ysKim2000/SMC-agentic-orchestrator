## 설명
Dual-head ECG transformer for six diagnostic and four rhythm groups. Input: heart ECG (12-lead, 10 s); file formats csv. Assesses: STEMI, ST depression, LBBB, RBBB, LVH, atrial fibrillation, bradycardia, tachycardia. Outputs: diagnostic and rhythm probabilities.
